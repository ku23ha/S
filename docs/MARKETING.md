# Launch and marketing kit

Written 23 November 2026. Christmas is 32 days away, and the Indian wedding season (November to February) is running now. Use both.

## 1. Positioning (use these words everywhere)

**One line:** The gift only one person can have.

**What it is, in one breath:** Every "World's Best ___" title can belong to exactly one person. You check if it's free, give it to someone you love, and they get a numbered certificate page that nobody else can ever have.

**Who buys:** people who want a small, personal, slightly funny extra for a partner, parent, sibling or friend, and want it in two minutes.

**Why they pay instead of making a free certificate:** the exclusivity is real. One holder, publicly recorded, forever. A Canva certificate can be made by anyone for anyone; this one can't.

**Tone:** warm, specific, a little playful. Never hype. Never pretend it's an award.

### Things we never do (they kill trust, and some break the rules in CLAUDE.md)
- No fake urgency, fake countdowns or "only 3 left". Scarcity here is real and needs no exaggeration.
- No fake holders, fake reviews or inflated counters. The registry starts empty and says so.
- Never call it official, certified, legal or accredited.
- No religious framing. Don't sell titles "in God's name" or call payments donations. Mixing faith and payment reads as manipulative, invites complaints, and "donation" would misdescribe a purchase. If someone wants a devotional title for themselves (World's Best Bhakt), the normal rules apply. We just don't market it that way.

## 6. Numbers to watch (PostHog and /admin)
- **Search rate:** visitors who search at least once. Healthy: above 50%.
- **Claim start rate:** searches that open the claim form. Healthy: above 8%.
- **Paid conversion:** visitors who pay. Healthy at launch: 1 to 2%.
- **Share rate:** certificates with 3 or more outside views in 7 days. Healthy: above 40%.
- **Loop purchases:** purchases that began on someone's certificate. Healthy: 2 or more per 10 claims.

### Stop or change course if (from the plan you started with)
1. Day 7: fewer than 1,000 visitors despite doing everything above.
2. Day 14: fewer than 20 paid claims from strangers, or conversion under 0.5% on 2,000+ visitors.
3. Day 21: fewer than 1 in 10 purchases arriving from a certificate page (the loop isn't working).
4. 26 December: under ₹10,000 net. Leave it running at near-zero cost for Valentine's Day and review on 15 February.

## 7. What not to spend money on
- No paid ads until conversion is proven above 1% on organic traffic.
- No influencer fees. Give free titles instead.
- No more features until the numbers above say people want this.
