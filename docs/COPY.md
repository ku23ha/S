# Copy

## Disclaimer line (exact; on every page, certificate image and email)
A novelty registry for fun and affection. Titles have no legal or official status.

Never use the words official, legal, certified or accredited in user-facing copy, except inside that line.

## Product name
The World's Best Registry. On certificates: "Entered in The World's Best Registry, No. 000482".

## Headline (home)
Every 'World's Best' title can belong to one person. Is yours taken?

## How it works (three steps)
1. Search. Type a title after "World's Best" and see if it is free.
2. Claim. Enter who it is for, add a dedication if you like, and pay once.
3. Share. They get a numbered certificate page that is theirs alone.

## Certificate page prompt
Is your title taken?

## Search states
- Reserved: "Someone is claiming this right now; try again in 15 minutes."
- Database unreachable: "Try again in a moment."

## Refund and removal policy
- If two people pay for the same title, the later payment is refunded automatically and the buyer is emailed free variants.
- Entries flagged by moderation show "in review". If rejected, the payment is refunded within 24 hours.
- Reported pages that break the rules are removed within 48 hours with a refund.
- Anyone named on a page can ask for it to be removed.
- A removed title becomes free again. Its registry number is retired and never reused.
- The buyer can fix a typo in the recipient name, from line or dedication within 24 hours using the manage link in their email.
